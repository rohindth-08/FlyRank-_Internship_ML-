## 3. The Pressure-Test Prompt

*Instructions: Paste this prompt into your newly configured Claude Project, hit enter, and take a screenshot of the conversation for your deliverable.*

**Your Prompt:**
> Here is my proposed portfolio sitemap:
> 1. Home (Hero statement + Call to Action)
> 2. Case Studies (Highlighting my FlyRank ML Capstone)
> 3. About Me (Short bio)
> 4. Contact (Links to Github/LinkedIn)
> 
> Please pressure-test this sitemap against my proof statement and my one action (getting hired). Is there any page missing that I absolutely need, or any page here that I should cut?

### Expected Output & Reflection

*Claude will likely tell you that the sitemap is beautifully lean, but it might suggest tying the "About Me" page more closely to the Case Studies rather than making it a standalone page.*

**Your Reflection (The "one thing I'll change"):**
> *Based on the pressure-test, I realized my "Contact" page doesn't need to be a separate page at all. I will move the Contact links and the "Let's discuss your content queue" CTA to the footer of every page so the user can take action from anywhere, making the sitemap even leaner.*
