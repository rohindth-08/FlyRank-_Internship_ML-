## 3. The Identity Kit

### Fonts
- **Heading Font:** `Inter` (Clean, modern, highly legible).
- **Body Font:** `Roboto` (Neutral, highly readable at small sizes).

### Palette (Hex Codes)
- **Background:** `#FAFAFA` (Warm near-white)
- **Text:** `#1A1A1A` (Soft near-black; gentler contrast than pure black/white)
- **Accent:** `#2E7D32` (A calm, professional green for links and buttons, fitting for data/growth)
